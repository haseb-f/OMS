### Verified clean (no finding)

- **Money formatter parity.** `formatMoney` is now a wrapper over `formatAmount`. Its output matches HEAD for finite values, NaN and negatives. The only difference is that a value that rounds to zero no longer prints `-0.00`. Report cells switched from browser-locale `toLocaleString(undefined)` to en-US, as intended.
- **Report figures.** `signed:false` removed from cash flow affected color only. Net loss is shown as an absolute value under a "Net Loss" label on screen, in export and in print. Section headings hide their amounts only when a `:total` row exists; the API `wrapSection` always adds one, and `cash-availability-tab.tsx` adds its own. TB closing Dr/Cr matches the BS signs (AR 452,745.00 Cr ↔ -452,745.00).
- **Totals math.** `document-totals-math.ts` is identical to HEAD's inline code: journal tolerance 0.001, `max(amount - allocated, 0)`, and the discount sign and shipping rule.
- **Permission gates.** A token-level diff over all removed and added lines found everything preserved, including landed-cost approve/confirm/cancel now in `canRun`, investor tabs, and partner reports still under `PermissionGate`.
- **Acceptance-script selectors.** 22 flagged strings were all false positives: data values, regexes, or `alertdialog`, which is still AlertDialog. `data-row`/`data-col`, `#payment-reject-reason`, `[cmdk-*]`, `data-sidebar`, `main div.rounded-sm.border` (matching-tab unchanged) and `main table tbody tr` all still resolve.
- **Icon-only buttons.** Every `size="icon*"` button has an `aria-label`. The 3 regex hits were false positives.
- **No remaining bad CSS in `apps/web/src`.** No gradients or `backdrop-blur`, no palette colors outside print pages, no physical left/right utilities outside shadcn `sidebar.tsx` side variants, and no raw `DialogContent` outside the shared modal and confirm.
- **Interaction checks** (desktop-ar-light and mobile 390):
  - MasterData "إضافة" dialog, ConfirmationDialog (archive → cancel) and product browser all fit the viewport, and their footer actions are visible.
  - The mobile Filters sheet fits (footer at 772–844).
  - EntityCombobox and SelectFilter: ArrowDown opens, Enter selects, Escape closes, and the popover stays in the viewport.
  - `/store-orders` sticky header holds after scrolling the grid 438px (`th` top unchanged at 197.5).
  - RTL pagination arrows flip correctly.

## Verdict

**Not ready for release.** The shared-system adoption is broad and consistent: 0 page-level overflow across
326 captures, dialogs fit, keyboard selection works, and money and totals math are unchanged. However:

- R1 removes visible keyboard focus app-wide, which is an accessibility regression (blocker).
- R2 hides account names in the General Ledger and statements on desktop, which clips essential financial data.
- R3 collapses the journal editor into cards on desktop.
- R4 leaves Arabic date and money reordering on major lists.

Fix R1–R4 and re-run this sweep before the Production pass. R5–R11 should land in this milestone.
