# Grid-card palettes per entity (R9 correction) — real business states only

A card's SURFACE carries ONE colour: the record's **primary workflow state**, read from the SAME
status → tone map its table badge uses (file named per row below). Everything else on the card is a
separate, labelled badge. A record with no workflow status is a **neutral card** — a status is never
invented to colour a surface. Tones are the five semantic ones (`--insight-*`: success = green,
warning = amber, info = blue — kept quiet, destructive = red, neutral = grey); they are never brand
decoration. The state is always also written as a badge with a label (and an icon where the entity
has one), so colour is never the only carrier.

Surface recipe (flat, no gradient): translucent tint (`10 %` of the tone, `14 %` on hover) over the card
surface, 1px hairline in the tone (`38 %`), thin inner highlight, one very small shadow.
Neutral = `5 %` tint, grey hairline. Selected = blue 2px ring on a neutral blue tint (never a status
colour). Contrast of text on the strongest wash is checked in `scripts/design/contrast-check.mjs`.

Interpretation notes (not invented states): "Scheduled" and similar words in requests map to the states
the data actually has; an invoice surface is its DOCUMENT status (a confirmed-but-unpaid invoice is a
green card with a red "unpaid" badge — the two answers stay separate).
